3 · Sepolia trial — real user-signed flow (testnet, no value)

Base Sepolia (84532). A maker wallet opens a strategy and a taker wallet triggers it — every step signed by you. The maker only grants an allowance; nothing is transferred out of your wallet until a taker fills.
Strategy template — what the maker actually opens • pair: USDC / dUSDT (both Base-Sepolia test tokens) • curve: PeggedSwap with virtual X0 / Y0 = 10 / 10 tokens — a pricing curve, not a deposit • commission: e.g. 0.30%, routed to your fee receiver • salt: a random nonce so every strategy is unique • maker: your wallet — it keeps custody; it only grants an allowance to Aqua How the process completes 1) Maker approves + ships → an on-chain "maker position" opens (no funds move). 2) Taker (a second wallet) swaps against it → taker pays tokenIn, receives tokenOut. 3) Your commission is charged inside the swap and lands in the fee receiver. Reference run (deployment doc): taker −9 USDC / +8.95 dUSDT · fee receiver +0.0045 USDC · maker +9 USDC / −8.95 dUSDT. No guaranteed APY. Testnet only, no value.
